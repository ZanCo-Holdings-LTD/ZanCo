RevSense FAQ

What is RevSense? RevSense listens to a noise your car is making and suggests what might be causing it, with a confidence score and a summary you can show a mechanic. It is a first step, not a replacement for having the car looked at.
Does RevSense record my audio? Recordings are analysed to produce your result and are not shared with anyone else. The privacy policy sets out exactly what is kept and for how long.
How accurate are the confidence scores? They are a guide, not a diagnosis. The score reflects how closely your recording matches sounds the model has learned, and a high score still needs a mechanic to confirm it.
What devices does RevSense support? RevSense is being built for iPhone, iOS 16 or later. It is not in the App Store yet. Ask us if you would like to hear when it is.
