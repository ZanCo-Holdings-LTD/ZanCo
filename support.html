RevSense FAQ

What is RevSense? RevSense uses on-device machine learning to analyse vehicle sounds and suggest probable issues with confidence scores. It is designed as a helpful first step, not a replacement for a qualified mechanic.
Does RevSense record my audio? Audio is processed on-device. We do not upload, store, or share your audio recordings unless you explicitly opt in to a future diagnostic-sharing feature.
How accurate are the confidence scores? Confidence scores are indicative and based on pattern matching against known vehicle sound profiles. They should be treated as suggestions, not definitive diagnoses.
What devices does RevSense support? RevSense is available on iOS devices running iOS 16 or later.
