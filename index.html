About ZanCo

The Founder

Abdul Hussain is a Senior Reporting Analyst working across Epic (Cogito) reporting and analytics, using Python and SQL to turn complex NHS data into clear, actionable insight.

He supports King’s College Hospital NHS Foundation Trust and collaborates across the wider London healthcare ecosystem, with a particular focus on building robust datasets and dashboards across Clarity, Caboodle, Reporting Workbench, SlicerDicer, and Radar.

With a BSc in Physics (First Class) and an MSc in Data Science (Distinction), plus experience as a Machine Learning Engineer, Abdul brings a strong scientific foundation and practical modelling skills to ZanCo.

How we build

ZanCo’s operating model is deliberately boring (the best kind): clear specs, reusable systems, careful compliance, and product decisions grounded in evidence.

Shared standards

  • Single visual identity system (Z master mark + ZCo lockup).
  • Consistent design tokens (dark mode first, high legibility, minimalism).
  • Privacy by default (especially for audio, location, and user-generated data).
  • Documentation-first: specs, build logs, and repeatable workflows.

Venture lifecycle

  • Idea → validation → MVP → iteration → operational hardening.
  • Each venture earns its own brand only when it proves traction.
  • ZanCo remains the quiet parent: governance, capital discipline, long horizon.
