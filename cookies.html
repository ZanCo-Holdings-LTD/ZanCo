Cookie Policy

How this website and our apps use cookies. The short answer for the website: they don't.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

2. Our Current Use

This website, zancoholdings.com, is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. Its fonts and images are served from our own domain, so opening a page sends nothing to a third party.

3. Cookies in Our Apps

Our mobile app, RevSense, may use the following kinds of local storage:

Strictly Necessary Required for the apps to function, e.g. authentication tokens, session management. These cannot be disabled.
Functional Remember your preferences and settings (e.g. language, region, display preferences).
Analytics (if enabled in future) If we introduce analytics in the future, we will update this policy and request your consent before collecting any analytics data.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We do not use third-party cookies. If that changes, for example to take payments or embed content, we will update this policy first and ask for consent where the law requires it.

5. Managing Cookies

You can manage cookies through your browser settings or device settings. Note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect app functionality.

  • iOS: Settings > Safari > Clear History and Website Data
  • Browser: Check your browser's privacy or cookie settings

6. Changes to This Policy

We will update this policy if our use of cookies changes, and say so on this website or in the app.
